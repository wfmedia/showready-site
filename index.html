Support & Privacy

ShowReady is listing photography for real estate agents, from William & Fields Media. You shoot the house on your phone; we finish the photos and email them back.

Support

Email us and a person will answer.

photos@showready.media

We reply within one business day. If a shoot is time-sensitive, say so in the subject line.

Common questions

Where are my photos? They are emailed to the address you verified as soon as they are finished. If they have not turned up, check your spam or junk folder first — that is where they usually are.

My upload stopped. Nothing is lost. Open the listing and tap Upload again; it continues from where it stopped rather than starting over, even if the app was closed in between.

I need to change my email address. Sign out and sign in with the new address. Your finished photos go to the address you verified, so it cannot be edited without verifying the new one.

Need to contact us? Email photos@showready.media about anything at all — a shoot in progress, a question about your photos, or something that isn't working. Replying to your delivery email reaches us too.

Deleting your account

Email photos@showready.media from the address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my account.

We delete your account, your email address, and every photo you have sent us within 30 days, and confirm by email when it is done. Nothing is kept afterwards except the minimum billing records the law requires us to keep.

Privacy policy

Last updated 23 August 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address and name. The address is verified with a code, because it is where your finished photos are sent. Your name labels your shoots.
  • The photos you take or send us, and the listing address you type in, so the shoot can be named and delivered.
  • Capture details recorded by the app — which lens, how level the phone was, the exposure used. These are used to finish your photos and nothing else.

What we do not collect

  • No location data. The app never asks for it.
  • No contacts, no photo library access, no microphone.
  • No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party tracking.
  • No payment details. Payment is handled outside the app, by our payment processor.

How your photos are handled

Your photos are processed by our editing provider and stored privately. They are never sold, never published, and never shown to anyone but you.

Original files are kept for 14 days, then deleted. Finished photos are kept for 28 days, so there is time to ask for them again if the delivery email goes astray. After that both are gone, so save your finished photos somewhere of your own when they arrive.

Who else sees anything

Three companies process data on our behalf: Google (storage and the mail that delivers your photos), Railway (the server the app talks to) and Autoenhance (which finishes the photos). None has any right to use your data for their own purposes. We do not sell or share your information with anyone else.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for all of it to be deleted, by emailing photos@showready.media. We answer within 30 days.

Children

ShowReady is a tool for working real estate agents and is not directed at anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will email the address you signed up with before the change takes effect.
